
# EarthPulse4 — Explore Earth, Hear Its Story

### An Accessible, Multisensory Climate Data Experience

**EarthPulse4** is an interactive platform that transforms NASA's historical climate data into visual and auditory experiences.

Using NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP v4), EarthPulse4 allows users to explore Earth's temperature changes through interactive maps, a 3D globe, historical visualizations, and musical sounds.

### How It Works

EarthPulse4 connects climate data with interactive technology. It visualizes temperature anomalies across geographic locations and historical periods, while its sonification feature translates temperature values into piano-based sounds.

This creates an alternative way to explore and understand climate change through both sight and sound, making scientific information more engaging and accessible, particularly for people who are blind or visually impaired.
